# DASH WORLD BY ESC ERAY 🌍



### 🎮 About
Dash World by ESC Eray is a Geometry Dash inspired platformer built from scratch (im sorry, with ai...) . Pure cube gameplay with fixed collision, smooth camera, and 10 reworked levels.

No ship. No triple spikes. Just skill.

### 🕹️ How to Play
- **TAP / SPACE** - Jump
- **TAP in air near yellow orb** - Boost
- Land on top of blocks, avoid spikes

### ✨ Features
- ✅ 10 Reworked Cube-Only Levels (Easy → Insane)
- ✅ Fresh ID every reset - everyone starts as YOU but gets unique GD-XXXXXX
- ✅ Friends system - add friends with their GD-ID
- ✅ Custom icon colors
- ✅ Fixed collision - no more going through blocks
- ✅ Smooth camera
- ✅ Music toggle
- ✅ 100% vanilla HTML/CSS/JS - no libraries
